Privacy Policy

Effective September 17, 2026 · Last updated September 22, 2026 · 日本語

Weird (the "app") lets you record and share strange experiences — paranormal encounters, UFOs, urban legends — on a map, together with the place they happened. To do that, the app handles a small amount of information about you, including your location. This page explains what we collect, what becomes public, who else receives it, and how you can have it deleted.

Information stored on your device

The following is kept on your device and is not sent to our servers.

  • Your signed-in session
  • Your Apple user identifier (kept in the device's secure storage)
  • Whether you chose to continue without signing in
  • Whether you have seen the introductory screens
  • A cache of profile images, so they load faster

None of this is used for advertising or behavioral analysis. The app's display language follows your device's language setting; there is no language switch inside the app.

What becomes public

Posts in Weird can be read by anyone, including people who are not signed in. The following is public:

  • Posts: title, body, thoughts on the cause, photos, tag, weather, the date and time of the experience, the exact location (latitude and longitude), the date it was posted, its trust score, and statistics such as the view count and the number of similar experiences
  • Profiles: display name, user ID, bio, external links, avatar image, background image
  • Follows and followers

Post photos, avatars and background images are stored in a publicly readable location: anyone who knows the URL can open them.

When you post anonymously

An anonymous post hides exactly four things that identify you:

  • your account ID
  • your display name
  • your user ID
  • your avatar image

Anonymous posts are also left out of the list of your posts and your post count as other people see them.

However, the following stays public even for an anonymous post:

  • the location (the exact coordinates — they are not blurred or rounded)
  • the photos
  • the title, body and thoughts on the cause
  • the date and time of the experience

Because of this, the place or the contents of a photo can still point back to you. For somewhere you would rather not reveal, such as your home, consider not posting it, or dropping a pin on a different spot on the map instead of using your current location.

Location

  • Location is read only while the app is open, when the map is shown and when you create a post. The app does not track you in the background.
  • You can withdraw the location permission at any time in your device settings. Without it, you can still post by dropping a pin on the map.
  • As described above, the location attached to a post is published as exact coordinates.

Photos

  • Only the photos you choose are uploaded.
  • Photos are converted to JPEG before upload, which removes embedded metadata such as the capture time and GPS coordinates (Exif). Anything visible in the image itself, of course, remains.

How we protect your information

  • Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted.
  • The database uses row-level access control and column-level privileges, so information that should not be public cannot be read from outside.
  • Tokens issued by Apple are kept in encrypted storage.
  • Photos have their embedded metadata (Exif) stripped before upload.
  • On your device, the session is kept in ordinary storage and the Apple user identifier in the device's secure storage.

Retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account exists, and we have not set a fixed retention period. The one exception is the record of posts you open: a daily job tallies those records and deletes them, leaving only the total view count for each post.

You can delete your account yourself in the app: Settings → Delete account. See Deleting Your Account for the steps and the exact scope. If something prevents you from deleting it in the app, write to the address above.

Deleting your account deletes:

  • your posts and drafts
  • the record of posts you opened
  • your follows and followers, on both sides of the relationship
  • reports you submitted
  • your profile, account record and blocks
  • your avatar, background image and post photo files

Deletion cannot be undone.

What remains: reports that other people filed about you are kept, so that violations can still be handled.

To delete a single post, open the experience, swipe up to reveal the action row, tap ⋮ and choose Delete (if you cannot delete it from the app for some reason, email us at the address above). A deleted post disappears from view for other people, but the record itself remains in the database. Its photo files are deleted once 30 days have passed since the post was deleted (if we are handling a report about that post, we keep them until the report is dealt with). Deleting your account removes your posts and their photo files entirely.

If you subscribe to Weird+: deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Apple manages the subscription, so cancel it on your device under Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions.
