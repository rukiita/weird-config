Contact us

Everything reaches us at one address:

lukiitaengineer@gmail.com

Write to us about bugs, inappropriate posts, claims that something infringes your rights, requests to take a post down, and questions about your account. Bug reports are accepted by email only; reporting a bug from inside the app is planned for a future release.

Weird is run by one person. We cannot promise a reply to every message, and replies may take anywhere from a few days to a few weeks. Reports of inappropriate content and claims of infringement are the exception: we review them within 24 hours of receiving them, and act as described in our Terms of Service.

When you write to us about a problem in the app, these details help us find the cause:

  • Your device (for example, iPhone 15) and iOS version
  • The app version (shown as "Version" on the Weird page in the App Store)
  • Which screen you were on, what you did, and what happened
  • A screenshot, if you have one

Reporting an inappropriate post

You can report a post from inside the app. Open the experience, swipe up to reveal the action row, tap ⋮, and choose Report. Pick a reason — Privacy violation or Spam — add details if you want, and send. Reporting requires signing in. The person you report is never told who reported them.

If neither reason fits, say so in the details box, or email us at lukiitaengineer@gmail.com. You can also use that address to report a post that breaks the law or our Terms of Service, that harms someone, or that infringes your rights. When you email us, the following helps us find the post quickly:

  • The title of the post and the author's display name (or a note that it was posted anonymously)
  • Roughly where it sits on the map (a place name is enough)
  • A screenshot
  • What you believe is wrong with it

We review every reported post within 24 hours and remove the ones we find to be in violation. We also delete the account of the person who posted it, ending their access to the app. If you want to change what you see right away, you can also block the person — see below.

Search is not available

Filtered search is part of Weird+, a monthly subscription. Browsing the map, reading experiences, posting and saving drafts, your profile, and following people are all free and need no subscription.

I subscribed but search is still locked (restoring a purchase)

If you changed devices or reinstalled the app, you may need to restore your purchase. Tap the search icon at the top left of the map to open the Weird+ screen, then tap "Restore purchases". Your device must be signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

If nothing changes after restoring, please email us.

Canceling your subscription

You cancel in iOS Settings, not in the app:

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap Subscriptions → Weird+
  4. Tap Cancel Subscription

You can do the same at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. To stop the subscription from renewing, cancel at least 24 hours before the next renewal date. The same applies during the free trial: cancel at least 24 hours before it ends and you will not be charged. After canceling, Weird+ stays active until the end of the period you already paid for. Refunds are decided by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com).

What happens if I do not allow location access

The app still works, but the features that rely on your current position are limited.

Still availableBrowsing the map, reading experiences, posting a past experience or rumor by dropping a pin on the map, drafts, your profile, following people, and search (every filter except distance)
Not availablePosting from your current location, the distance filter in search, sorting by distance
Map on launchInstead of centering on you, the map opens wide enough to show all of Japan. At that zoom level the app loads no experiences, so you will see no pins at all. Pan and zoom in on an area you are curious about and the experiences there will appear

You can change your mind later in Settings → Weird → Location on your iPhone. Weird uses your location only while the app is open; it never tracks you in the background.

What is hidden when I post anonymously

Only who you are is hidden. The content of the post is published exactly as it would be otherwise.

HiddenYour display name, your user ID and your profile picture. Anonymous posts also stay out of the post list and the post count that other people see on your profile
Not hiddenThe location of the post (exact coordinates), photos, title, description, your thoughts on the cause, tags, weather, and the date and time of the experience

The location of an anonymous post appears on the map at its exact coordinates and is visible to anyone, including people who are not signed in. Neither the content nor the location can be changed after posting. Please do not post from your home, your workplace, or anywhere else you would rather keep private. When you post a past experience or a rumor, you can drop a pin on the map instead of using your current location.

Hiding someone from your view (blocking)

Open the person's profile and choose Block from the ⋮ menu to the left of the follow button. You need to be signed in to block someone.

  • Blocking hides that person's posts from your view
  • The person is not told that you blocked them. They will, however, notice two things: any follow relationship between you ends in both directions, and they can no longer follow you
  • To unblock, open your profile and go to Settings → Blocked users, then tap Unblock. You can do this at any time. Unblocking does not restore the follows that were removed
  • Anonymous posts carry no author information, so you cannot block their author from the post

Deleting or rewriting a post

You can delete your own posts from inside the app.

  • To delete a post, open the experience, swipe up to reveal the action row, tap ⋮ and choose Delete. If you cannot delete it from the app for some reason, email us at lukiitaengineer@gmail.com. A deleted post stops appearing to other users, but the record itself stays in our database. Its photo files are deleted once 30 days have passed since the post was deleted (if we are handling a report about that post, we keep them until the report is dealt with)
  • A published post cannot be changed. If you want to rewrite one, delete it as described above and post it again
  • To remove all of your posts at once, delete your account instead: that removes your posts and their photo files together

Deleting your account

You can do this yourself in the app. Tap the icon at the top right of the map to open your profile, then choose Settings → Delete account. Once you confirm, your posts, photos, profile and follow relationships are deleted, and this cannot be undone.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Weird+ subscription. Cancel it first, using the steps above.

For the full procedure and exactly what is removed, see Deleting Your Account.
