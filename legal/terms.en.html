About the App

Weird lets you record strange experiences — ghosts, UFOs, urban legends and the like — on a map, together with the location where they happened, and share them with other people.

Everything you read in the App was written by its users. We make no claim that any of it is true. The App exists for entertainment and for keeping a record; it does not prove or endorse the existence of any paranormal phenomenon.

This version of the App lets you:

  • browse the map and read the details of an experience (no sign-in needed);
  • post an experience — from where you are now, or as a past experience — and save drafts;
  • create and edit your profile (display name, user ID, bio, web link, avatar, background image);
  • follow other people, and see who follows whom;
  • report a post, and block or unblock another user;
  • search for experiences by the criteria you choose (for subscribers to the paid feature, Weird+).

Posts and what becomes public

When you post, the exact location of the experience is published along with it. Your post appears as a pin on the map and can be seen by anyone using the App, including people who are not signed in.

Posting anonymously hides information about the author only. The location, photos, text and date are published exactly as they are for any other post. There is no option to blur, round or shift the location.

For this reason, do not post from your home, workplace, school, or anywhere else you would not want others to find you. Do not post someone else's home, or a place that would reveal where a particular person can be found.

Photos are stored where anyone with the link can view them. Converting the image on upload removes Exif data such as the time and GPS coordinates of the shot, but whatever is visible in the photo itself — a nameplate, a license plate, a person's face — is published as it is.

How we handle objectionable content

We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and for abusive users. We remove posts that violate these Terms and eject the people who posted them from the App. You can protect yourself in the following ways:

  • Report: open an experience, swipe up to reveal the action row, and tap ⋮, then choose Report. Pick a reason — Privacy violation or Spam — add details if you want, and send. If neither reason fits, say so in the details box. Reporting requires signing in. The person you report is never told who reported them. You can also write to us at lukiitaengineer@gmail.com.
  • Block: open the person's profile and swipe up to expand it — ⋮ appears to the left of the follow button — then choose Block. Their posts disappear from your view, and any follow relationship between you ends in both directions. The other person is not notified.
  • Unblock: from your profile, go to Settings → Blocked users and tap Unblock. You can do this at any time. Unblocking does not restore the follows that were removed.

We review every report within 24 hours of receiving it and remove any post we find to be in breach of these Terms. We also delete the account of the person who posted it, ending their access to the App. Explaining our decision in detail, or finishing a case that requires looking into who holds which rights, can take longer than that. Where someone's privacy is involved, we may not be able to share the details of what we did.

When you post, we run an automated check on the title and body against a list of prohibited words. We are not obliged to monitor every post, but we review reported content within 24 hours as described above, and we act the same way on violations we find ourselves.
